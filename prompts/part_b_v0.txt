1. expertise_claim: the reviewer claims knowledge, skill or experience that
   gives their judgement more authority than an ordinary buyer's.
   TRUE for: an occupation or training ("as a seamstress", "I'm a stylist");
   long experience with the brand or category ("I've bought this brand for
   10 years"); specialist knowledge used as a reason ("as someone who sews").

   TRUE for: "I think", "maybe", "perhaps", "might", "probably", "seems",

   FALSE for: degree words describing the item ("a bit long", "slightly
   sheer"); confidently stated mixed or negative opinions; questions; strong
   confident language; "for me" or "on my body" unless they clearly soften
   a claim.

3. social_proof: the review cites other people's reactions to, or behaviour
   around, the product.
   TRUE for: compliments or approval from others ("I get compliments", "my
   husband loved it"); others buying or owning it ("my sister bought one");
   other reviewers' opinions; recommendations from others ("the store
   associate recommended it").
   FALSE for: the reviewer's own repeat purchases; advice to the reader
   ("you'll love it", "size down").
